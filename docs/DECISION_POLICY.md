## Reading

- θ is derived, never tuned: no score-maximising statistic appears in this module — the cost ratio alone moves the operating point.
- The policy works as designed: declined loans default at ~3-4x the rate of funded ones at every ratio, and every operating point beats fund-everyone.
- **Drift caveat (measured in #39):** validation scores under-predict by ~0.027 on average, so each θ declines fewer loans than intended. Shifted-score bound at 5:1: decline rate 48.8% vs 38.7% unshifted, expected cost 0.5997 vs 0.6103. This is a monitoring obligation for P10 (calibration drift), not a reason to hand-adjust θ.
