# ADR-0003: Cost matrix for the grant/decline decision

## Context

The operating threshold of a granting model is entirely determined by the relative cost
of its two error types, so the ratio must be written down BEFORE any model output exists
(otherwise the ratio quietly becomes "whatever makes the model look good"). This project
has no access to LendingClub's real unit economics, so the ratio is an assumption — the
requirement is that it be explicit, defensible in order of magnitude, and
sensitivity-tested, not that it be precisely true.

Scale anchor, measured on the training split: mean funded amount $12,715 (median
$10,000), default rate 14.07%.

## Decision

**FN : FP = 5 : 1 [ASSUMED]**, with sensitivity analysis over **3:1 to 8:1** reported in
P6 alongside any threshold-dependent result.

Order-of-magnitude reasoning (not a calibrated LGD study):

- **False negative** (fund a loan that defaults): loss ≈ outstanding principal at
  charge-off minus recoveries. Unsecured personal loans recover little, and charge-off
  typically happens mid-life with most principal outstanding — call it a substantial
  fraction of the ~$12.7k mean principal.
- **False positive** (decline a loan that would repay): loss ≈ forgone interest margin
  over a 36-month life — roughly the low-to-mid teens as a percent of principal in
  total, i.e. an order of magnitude smaller than an FN.
- The ratio of those two rough quantities lands in the mid single digits; 5:1 is the
  midpoint we adopt, and the 3:1–8:1 band brackets the reasoning's slack.

Under cost-minimising thresholding on calibrated probabilities, θ = C_FP / (C_FP + C_FN):
5:1 gives θ ≈ 0.167; the sensitivity band maps to θ ∈ [0.111, 0.250].

## Alternatives considered and rejected

- **Maximise F1 / accuracy for the threshold**: rejected — no economic meaning; treats
  FN and FP as equal, which is plainly false in lending, and is indefensible under
  questioning.
- **Literature-derived exact LGD/EAD figures**: rejected for now — false precision;
  numbers vary by product, era and workout policy, and adopting one unverified figure
  would dress an assumption up as a fact. This is exactly what the open research
  ticket exists to do properly.
- **Per-loan profit matrix using int_rate**: rejected — int_rate is
  BANNED_UNDERWRITING (Charter §1); building the cost matrix on it would re-import
  LendingClub's pricing model through the back door and shift the project toward a
  pricing framing.

## Consequences

Positive: the threshold becomes an economic derivation, not a tuning artifact; the
sensitivity band makes P6's conclusions robust to the assumption's slack.

Negative / to manage: the ratio is load-bearing and assumed. Mitigations: tagged
[ASSUMED] wherever used; sensitivity reported alongside every threshold-dependent
metric; an open research ticket (BACKLOG.md item 6) tracks replacing the assumption
with evidence — its outcome either confirms this ADR or supersedes it with ADR-000N.
