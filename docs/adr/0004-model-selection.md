## Context

Three baselines existed per P5 (prior, logistic regression, LightGBM), plus an
isotonic-calibrated LightGBM variant built for the P6 calibration assessment. The
selection rules were frozen pre-model in docs/EVAL_PROTOCOL.md: PR-AUC primary, on
validation (2016-H1, coverage 0.821), calibration must not degrade beyond noise,
ties go to the simpler model, and every compared run must be reproducible from its
run ID (all were; see docs/REPRODUCIBILITY.md).

## Decision

**LightGBM 4.7.0, uncalibrated, fixed zero-search parameters** — run
`e299b8e7489e4e9186b3680c39b45166` — is the v1 model, to be registered in P7.

Selection evidence (validation):

- PR-AUC 0.3446 vs logistic 0.3236. The margin is real, not noise: month-stratified
  bootstrap (500 resamples) gives **+0.0209, 95% CI [+0.0177, +0.0242]** — the
  interval excludes zero, so the simpler-model tie rule does not bind.
- Brier 0.1382 vs 0.1400 — calibration is not degraded by the choice.
- Expected cost 0.610 vs 0.625 FP-units/loan at the [ASSUMED] 5:1 matrix.

Confirmed on the holdout (2016-H2, opened once with the recorded acknowledgment,
coverage 0.604): PR-AUC 0.3767 vs logistic 0.3569; cost 0.701 vs 0.718; ranking
preserved. Full numbers: docs/P6_FINAL_REPORT.md.

## Alternatives considered and rejected

- **Logistic regression**: rejected on a bootstrap-real PR-AUC and cost margin.
  Remains the natural challenger; nothing in this ADR retires it from future
  comparisons.
- **Isotonic-calibrated LightGBM**: rejected on evidence (#39) — worse on every
  axis (Brier 0.1389, ECE 0.0329, PR-AUC 0.3358 on validation). Structural reason
  recorded: the observed miscalibration is base-rate drift, which a
  within-training-window calibrator cannot learn, while the scheme pays six months
  of training data and the entire 2015+ bureau block for the attempt.
- **Prior**: the floor, kept for legibility of gains.
- **Hyperparameter search over any of the above**: out of scope by Charter
  non-goal 8; the zero-search parameter set is part of what was selected.

## Consequences

Positive: best measured ranking and cost at every examined operating point;
reproducible bit-exact from its run ID; served by the same single pipeline.

Negative / accepted with eyes open:
- **The model under-predicts under drift** (validation mean 0.157 vs observed
  0.184; holdout ECE 0.064 vs validation 0.027 — worsening with time). Accepted
  because no within-window fix exists (#39); the obligation transfers to P10
  monitoring (calibration drift is a first-class replay metric) and P11 retraining.
  The model card must state this.
- Expected-cost figures inherit ADR-0003's [ASSUMED] 5:1 ratio (band reported
  everywhere; revision tracked in issue #70).
- Tree-model opacity relative to logistic: accepted for v1; slice report (#41)
  stands in as the behavioural documentation.

## Review

Superseded naturally by the first P11-approved retrain, or revisited if issue #70
lands a cost ratio outside the 3:1–8:1 band this selection was examined under.
